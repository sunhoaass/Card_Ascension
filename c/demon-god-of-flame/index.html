PassiveGod of Demons

Demon God of Flame's blue flames consume everything. Each hit sets every enemy card on fire for 2 turns (5% of its max HP per turn). Each hit adds 3 Burn stacks (each stack: 0.9% of the enemy's max HP per turn, max 4 stacks, 3 turns). Every enemy card starts with 4.6% less damage. 6.8% chance per hit to Confuse the enemy (it skips its next turn and hits itself for half damage). Can't be burned.
